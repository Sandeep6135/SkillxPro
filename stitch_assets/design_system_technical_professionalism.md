# Design System - Technical Professionalism
Aesthetic: Corporate / Modern (Material Design 3)
Radii: 24px (rounded-3xl) for cards, pill-shaped for buttons, 8px for inputs.

## Colors
- Main Background: Light Grey (#F1F3F4)
- Surfaces: White (#FFFFFF)
- Typography: Deep Charcoal (#202124)
- Primary Blue: #4285F4 (Actions)
- Success Green: #34A853 (Completions)
- Warning Yellow: #FBBC04
- Danger Red: #EA4335
